== Overview

The general-purpose building blocks: delays and sliding windows, simple
filters and smoothers, gates, detectors and triggers, and a few shapers.
Most are small function objects that take a sample and return one, so they
compose freely with each other and with the rest of Q.

== Delays and Windows

[cols="1,3"]
|===

| xref:reference/misc/delay.adoc[Delay]
| Fixed and fractional delay lines, with feedback for echoes.

| xref:reference/misc/sample_hold.adoc[Sample Hold]
| The value a signal had about a duration ago, without a delay line.

| xref:reference/misc/moving_sum.adoc[Moving Sum]
| The running sum of a sliding window of samples.

| xref:reference/misc/moving_average.adoc[Moving Average]
| The mean of a sliding window: the simplest FIR low pass.

| xref:reference/misc/moving_maximum.adoc[Moving Maximum]
| The largest value within a sliding window.

|===

== Filters and Smoothing

[cols="1,3"]
|===

| xref:reference/misc/one_pole_lowpass.adoc[One Pole Low Pass]
| The basic 6 dB/octave smoothing filter.

| xref:reference/misc/dc_block.adoc[DC Block]
| A high pass that removes a DC offset.

| xref:reference/misc/dynamic_smoother.adoc[Dynamic Smoother]
| A self-modulating low pass: smooth when a value is still, quick when it moves.

| xref:reference/misc/integrator.adoc[Integrator]
| A running sum of the scaled input.

| xref:reference/misc/median3.adoc[Median]
| A 3-point median filter that rejects lone outliers.

| xref:reference/misc/fast_downsample.adoc[Fast Downsample]
| Halves the sample rate with cheap antialiasing.

| xref:reference/misc/differentiator.adoc[Differentiators]
| First and central differences and slope: how fast a signal changes.

| xref:reference/misc/allpass.adoc[All-Pass Phase Shifters]
| One-pole and polyphase all-pass sections that shape only the phase.

| xref:reference/misc/hilbert_quadrature.adoc[Hilbert Quadrature]
| Two outputs 90 degrees apart across the audio band: the analytic signal.

|===

== Gates

[cols="1,3"]
|===

| xref:reference/misc/noise_gate.adoc[Noise Gate]
| Mutes a signal below a threshold, with hysteresis.

| xref:reference/misc/onset_gate.adoc[Onset Gate]
| A noise gate with a slope guard that gates out slow attacks.

| xref:reference/misc/delta_gate.adoc[Delta Gate]
| True when a signal stands a ratio above its value a duration ago.

|===

== Detectors and Triggers

[cols="1,3"]
|===

| xref:reference/misc/zero_crossing.adoc[Zero Crossing]
| A pulse that is high for the positive part of each cycle.

| xref:reference/misc/schmitt_trigger.adoc[Schmitt Trigger]
| A comparator with hysteresis.

| xref:reference/misc/window_comparator.adoc[Window Comparator]
| A comparator with explicit high and low thresholds.

| xref:reference/misc/edge.adoc[Edge Detectors]
| Rising and falling edges as single-sample events.

| xref:reference/misc/monostable.adoc[Monostable]
| One-shot pulse generators, plain and retriggerable.

| xref:reference/misc/peak.adoc[Peak]
| Pulses at the peaks of a waveform.

| xref:reference/misc/peak_picker.adoc[Peak Picker]
| Finds a signal's maxima and keeps the meaningful ones.

| xref:reference/misc/spectral_flux.adoc[Spectral Flux]
| The level of the part of a signal above a cutoff frequency.

|===
