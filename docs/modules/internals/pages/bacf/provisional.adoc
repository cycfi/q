= bacf_provisional

A note's first estimate of its lag, before any lock, for the
xref:bacf/lag_tracker.adoc[BACF lag tracker]. The tracker gives it out as
`provisional()`; the pitch shifter restarts a pick's read on it.

== Include

```c++
#include <q/pitch/detail/bacf_provisional.hpp>
```

== Declaration

```c++
namespace cycfi::q::detail
{
   class bacf_provisional
   {
   public:

      static constexpr float        strong      = 0.6f;
      static constexpr int          fractions   = 4;
      static constexpr float        agree       = 0.02f;
      static constexpr float        repeat      = 0.85f;

                           bacf_provisional(
                              std::uint32_t min_lag
                            , std::uint32_t max_lag
                            , float long_hint
                           );

                           template <typename Pulses>
      void                 estimate(
                              Pulses const& pulses, bacf_slice const& slice
                           );

                           template <typename Periodicity>
      void                 vet(
                              bacf_slice const& slice, std::uint32_t now
                            , Periodicity const& periodicity
                           );

      bool                 estimated() const;
      bool                 pending() const;
      float                value() const;
      void                 drop();
      void                 reset();
   };
}
```

== The estimate

`estimate(pulses, slice)` runs at an unlocked edge, until there is an
estimate. The latest pulse must be after the drop and strong, at least
`strong` of the slice's peak. Its estimate is the span to the nearest like
pulse before it that is also after the drop, within the lag range (a span
under `min_lag` is passed over; past `max_lag` the search stops). A pair of
like pulses exists about two periods into a note, before the lock: on the
Low E test note, 15 ms after the pick against 40 ms.

== The chord check

The estimate rests on one pair of like pulses. On a single note the pair
spans the note's period. On a chord it may not: at a re-strum the first pair
can come from one note of the chord, and the chord as a whole does not
repeat at that note's period, so a splice on it joins mismatched material.
In a strummed guitar recording shifted up 12 semitones, a re-strum gave an
estimate of 5.03 ms, one note's period, and the shifter spliced the chord
every 5 ms for 42 ms, until the lock found the chord's 24.2 ms. It was heard
as a thud.

The check applies only after a chord, which the slice's hint shows: a hint
(the last good lag before the drop) over `long_hint`, 13 ms in the tracker.
13 ms is above a guitar's low E period (12.1 ms), so a low single note does
not count as a chord, and below a chord's common period (24.3 ms for an E
chord). After a shorter hint, or none, the estimate stands at once: checking
every estimate cost single notes 1.3 ms of latency at +12 semitones, for
estimates that were already right.

After a long hint the estimate stands when either holds:

* **It agrees with the hint.** The hint is 1 to `fractions` (4) times the
  estimate, within `agree` (2%). A chord's common period is a whole multiple
  of the periods of its notes that divide it, so a re-strum of the same
  chord whose pair spans the chord's period, or a clean fraction of it such
  as its lowest note's, passes at once. The 5.03 ms above does not: 24.2 /
  5.03 is 4.81.
* **It repeats.** Once one lag of the slice can be scored
  (`slice.ready()`), the bits over that lag match the lag before at `repeat`
  (85%) or more (`periodicity(estimate)`). A single note does. One note of a
  chord does not: in the same recording, the chord's bitstream at a half to a
  quarter of its period matches 50 to 70%, noise 50%. One lag is the
  earliest a repeat can be scored after the drop; the full window would
  wait until 11.6 ms of the slice can be scored, the latency the estimate is
  there to save.

`vet()` runs at the end of each sample while an estimate is `pending()`.
Until the estimate stands, `value()` is 0 and the shifter's restart waits
for the lock or its latency cap.

== Tests

`test/pitch/detail/bacf_provisional.cpp`: the first strong pair after the
drop, with earlier pulses ignored; only pulses after the drop pairing; a weak
latest pulse giving none; lags out of range; after a long hint, an estimate
that agrees standing at once (3 and 4 times it); one that does not, waiting
for one lag, then standing only once it repeats; `drop()` and `reset()`.
